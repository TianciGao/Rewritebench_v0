# No Leaderboard Boundary

No global leaderboard is created or authorized by this packet.

Track A 120 and PostgreSQL-only PG40 evidence cannot be directly ranked because they have different scopes, denominators, engines, and route semantics.

Verifier support is not a rewrite-generation baseline. SQLSolver and VeriEQL support status must remain separate from route-card metrics and official SER claims.

`tag_slices` are not POCR. Source-like diagnostics are not ranking metrics. Failure buckets are not SER. Local checker exactness is not formal verifier evidence.

Speedup is interpreted only over exact + timed rows. Missing timing is not zero, and non-exact rows are timing-ineligible.

The denominator is unchanged. Case membership is unchanged. Paper results are unchanged. Raw legacy evidence is unchanged.
