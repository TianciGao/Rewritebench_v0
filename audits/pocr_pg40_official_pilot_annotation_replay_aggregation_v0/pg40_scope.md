# PG40 Scope

This is not official POCR.

No route-level official POCR score is emitted.

No paper-facing metric is promoted.

POCR@planned and POCR@candidate remain D039 promotion views. POCR@curated remains deferred until a predeclared curated manifest exists. Micro-average is diagnostic only and not the paper formula.

PG40 is not Track A 120.

Scope: Common-core v0, PostgreSQL only, 40 planned rows per route, four routes, 160 planned route rows total. PG40 follows the tri-engine pilot because it is the next bounded denominator where existing Repair-1 and no-op evidence can be replayed and SQLGlot optimize missing-row fail-closed handling can be tested before any Track A 120 expansion.

Routes remain separate: Direct LLM original and Repair-1 are distinct LLM routes; SQLGlot no-op and SQLGlot optimize are distinct routes. SQLGlot no-op candidates were not used as SQLGlot optimize substitutes. SQLGlot optimize missing candidates are retained as no-candidate fail-closed rows for POCR@planned and excluded from POCR@candidate.
