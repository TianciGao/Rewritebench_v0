# Diagnostic Vs Official Boundary

Annotation JSONL is diagnostic evidence only.

Stage A annotation alone is not counted.

Stage B transformation-aware validation is diagnostic only.

No official POCR is computed.

No route-level POCR score is emitted.

No paper-facing metric is promoted.

No global leaderboard is produced.

Generating or replaying annotation JSONL does not update top-level `reports/` or `results/`, does not promote retained evidence, does not change denominators, does not change case membership, and does not change paper results.
